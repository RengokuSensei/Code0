Completed my Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Zoology. Studying living organisms gifted me with a fundamental appreciation for biological systems, evolutionary history, anatomical design, cellular bio-energetics, and ecological dynamics. Nature is the ultimate engineering teacher.
